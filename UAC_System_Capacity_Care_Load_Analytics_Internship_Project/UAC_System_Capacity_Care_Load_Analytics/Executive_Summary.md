# Executive Summary

## System Capacity & Care Load Analytics for Unaccompanied Children

### Purpose
A centralized decision-support dashboard for understanding the UAC care pipeline from CBP custody through HHS care and discharge.

### Dataset
- Reporting period: **12 Jan 2023 – 21 Dec 2025**
- Populated reporting dates: **720**
- Full calendar span: **1,075 days**
- Source rows: **1,170**
- Blank source rows: **450**

### Key decision-support questions
- What is the current and peak combined care load?
- Is HHS inflow exceeding discharge?
- When does pressure become sustained?
- Which periods show elevated stress?
- How volatile is the flow system?

### Recommended workflow
1. Review System Load Overview.
2. Compare CBP and HHS trajectories.
3. Inspect rolling intake pressure.
4. Review high-stress periods.
5. Check data-quality exceptions.
6. Combine analytical signals with official capacity, staffing and clinical information.

### Governance
This prototype supports situational awareness and planning. It does not determine individual risk, legal status, clinical acuity, eligibility or actual facility capacity.
